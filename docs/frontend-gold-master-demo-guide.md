# Gold Master Demo Guide

## Demo Narrative

NEXORA AI OS helps a business owner create an adaptive AI operating workspace, understand the business quickly and act from one command center.

## Presenter Checklist

- Start from the public landing page.
- Show the premium enterprise identity and value proposition.
- Open signup and explain secure account creation.
- Walk through the AI-guided onboarding flow.
- Generate or open the workspace command center.
- Show the adaptive sidebar and breadcrumbs.
- Use global search to find invoices, employees, reports and AI agents.
- Open Ctrl/Cmd K and show universal search plus AI command handoff.
- Open notifications and explain approvals, risks and AI recommendations.
- Open unified settings and review workspace, access, notification, AI and capability sections.
- Open AI Copilot and ask for an executive summary.
- Sign out from the profile menu.

## Happy Path

1. Land on `/`.
2. Open `/auth/signup`.
3. Continue to `/onboarding`.
4. Open `/workspace-launcher`.
5. Search for `Invoices`.
6. Open `/settings`.
7. Toggle AI Copilot from the top navigation.
8. Sign out from the profile menu.

## Demo Data Notes

The frontend is ready for seeded backend data. Avoid promising live production integrations unless staging provider credentials are configured.

## Recovery Notes

If a live backend is unavailable during the demo, keep the walkthrough focused on the production frontend shell, generated workspace context, universal search, settings and AI Copilot experience.
