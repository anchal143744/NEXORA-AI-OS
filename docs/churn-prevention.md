
# Churn Prevention

Churn prevention in Phase 40 combines new revenue-level churn signals with existing customer success churn prediction records. This lets NEXORA understand both raw signals and scored customer risk.

## Capabilities

- Signal capture for usage decline, sponsor changes, unresolved service issues and commercial risk.
- Existing ChurnPrediction reuse for probability, risk band, model version and prevention actions.
- Account health scoring and recommendations for customer success and revenue teams.
- RevenueRisk reuse for at-risk value, risk drivers and mitigation actions.

## API Surface

- /v1/churn-intelligence
- /v1/account-health
- /v1/revenue-risk
