
# Procurement Center

Phase 41 adds an enterprise procurement center for NEXORA AI. It manages purchase categories, procurement requests, approvals, purchase orders, procurement events and budget-aware workflow control.

## Capabilities

- Purchase request creation with vendor, category, department, amount, priority and required-by metadata.
- Procurement approval workflow records with approver, level, status, risk and policy snapshot.
- Purchase order management connected to requests, vendors, categories and spend transactions.
- Procurement event stream for audit, realtime updates, workflow automation and integration routing.
