# Phase 45 Asset Analytics Report

Version: 0.45.0
Date: 2026-06-14

## Analytics Coverage

- Asset health score through condition, reliability and utilization factors.
- Asset risk classification through health score and failure probability.
- Total cost of ownership through acquisition, maintenance, downtime and disposal costs.
- Maintenance backlog through open work orders and technician capacity.
- Warranty and contract expiry monitoring through expiresAt and renewal reminders.
- Work order execution tracking through tasks, parts, time entries and approvals.
- IoT monitoring through devices, telemetry, alerts and sensor calibration records.
- Reliability and utilization analytics through asset metrics, snapshots, reliability scores and utilization records.
- AI asset recommendations through AssetCopilotQuery, AssetRecommendation and AssetInsight records.

- calculateAssetHealthScore
- classifyAssetRisk
- calculateTotalCostOfOwnership
- calculateMaintenanceBacklog
