# Billing Center

Phase 39 adds the external billing experience for customers.

## Capabilities

- Subscription center with plans, entitlements, renewals and billing cycles.
- Billing profiles with tax, address and payment terms metadata.
- Invoice records with line items, tax breakdowns, payments and due tracking.
- Payment method records with provider, type, verification and default state.
- Usage metrics for billing, usage monitoring and customer analytics.

## API Groups

- /v1/subscriptions
- /v1/billing
- /v1/invoices
- /v1/payments
