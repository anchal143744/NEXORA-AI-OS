# Trust Center

Phase 38 adds the enterprise Trust Center for external customer assurance.

## Capabilities

- Trust reports with visibility, publication and expiration controls.
- Security certifications with authorities, scopes and evidence URLs.
- Compliance artifacts with framework, classification and access rules.
- Customer-facing assurance evidence connected to ecosystem events.

## Database

- TrustReport
- SecurityCertification
- ComplianceArtifact
