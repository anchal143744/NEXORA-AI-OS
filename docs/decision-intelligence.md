# Decision Intelligence

Version: 0.32.0

Decision Intelligence records AI decisions, reasons, risk levels, policy checks and human review status for explainable enterprise automation.

## Capabilities

- AI decision recording with model, version and deployment references.
- Decision reasons with weighted evidence.
- Decision risks with severity, probability, impact and mitigation.
- Decision dashboards for risk and review oversight.

## API Groups

- /v1/decision-intelligence
- /v1/ai-explainability

## Database Models

- AIDecision
- AIDecisionReason
- AIDecisionRisk
- AIExplainabilityRecord
