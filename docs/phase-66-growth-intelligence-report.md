# Phase 66 Growth Intelligence Report

## Scope

Phase 66 adds the post-launch Growth Program workspace for executive visibility into customer expansion, adoption, revenue growth, retention and global enterprise scale.

## Growth Command Center

- Active Organizations
- Active Users
- Revenue Growth
- Expansion Revenue
- Churn Risk
- Adoption Trends

## Executive Readout

The Growth Command Center keeps the launched product accountable to customer adoption, revenue expansion and enterprise health after the public launch. It connects customer success, sales, AI expansion and global readiness into one executive-grade operating layer.
