# Customer Success Workspace

Phase 39 integrates customer success into the customer-facing portal.

## Capabilities

- Reuses the existing CustomerSuccessPlan model and /v1/customer-success API group.
- Connects customer account health and lifecycle stage to success workflows.
- Supports follow-up, adoption monitoring, engagement scoring and AI recommendations from the existing customer success foundation.
- Provides a frontend Customer Success Workspace panel in the customer portal experience.
