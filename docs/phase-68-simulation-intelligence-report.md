# Phase 68 Simulation Intelligence Report

## Scope

The simulation center covers Growth Scenarios, Revenue Changes, Hiring Plans, Budget Adjustments, Product Launches and Market Expansion.

## Enterprise Controls

- Tenant-scoped and workspace-scoped assumptions remain aligned with NEXORA architecture.
- Scenario outputs are executive decision support, not uncontrolled autonomous execution.
- Strategic plans should continue through approval, audit and governance workflows.
- Digital twin signals are designed for board-level clarity with traceable assumptions.
