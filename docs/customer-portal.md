# Customer Portal

Phase 39 adds the customer-facing portal foundation.

## Capabilities

- Customer accounts with account health, lifecycle stage, success tier and external contacts.
- Customer workspaces with access levels, users and permissions.
- Customer portal dashboard for account, workspace, subscription and billing visibility.
- Existing CustomerSuccessPlan model and /v1/customer-success APIs remain integrated for customer success workflows.

## Database

- CustomerAccount
- CustomerWorkspace
- CustomerSuccessPlan reused from Phase 28
