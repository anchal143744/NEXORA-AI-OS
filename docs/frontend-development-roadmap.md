# Frontend Development Roadmap

NEXORA AI backend is now ready for full frontend execution.

## Frontend Phases

1. Auth, onboarding and business setup.
2. Adaptive workspace generation UI.
3. Enterprise shell deep navigation and command palette expansion.
4. Core operating modules: CRM, finance, HR, projects, documents and analytics.
5. Workflow builder, AI copilot and agent console.
6. Industry operations: supply chain, manufacturing, workforce, EAM and field service.
7. Executive layers: planning, MDM, global connectivity, governance and production readiness.
8. Mobile, offline and responsive enterprise refinement.
9. Accessibility, localization, performance and QA hardening.
10. Launch readiness UX polish and customer onboarding content.
