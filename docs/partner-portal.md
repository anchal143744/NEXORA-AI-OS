# Partner Portal

Phase 39 adds partner self-service infrastructure.

## Capabilities

- Partner accounts with tier, commission rate and portal access metadata.
- Partner workspaces with users, permissions and portal roles.
- Partner commissions with source, earned, paid and status tracking.
- Partner referrals with submitted, converted and customer account linkage.

## API Groups

- /v1/partner-portal
- /v1/partner-referrals
