
# Vendor Management

Vendor management centralizes supplier profiles, contracts, scorecards and risk context. Vendor records support country, tier, owner, category and capability metadata for global business operations.

## Entities

- Vendor
- VendorContract
- VendorScorecard

## Controls

All vendor records are tenant-scoped, workspace-aware, soft-deletable and indexed for status, risk, type, owner and renewal queries.
