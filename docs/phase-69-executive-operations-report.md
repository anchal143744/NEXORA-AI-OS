# Phase 69 Executive Operations Report

## Scope

Executive Operations Copilot supports Show operational health, Predict failures, Analyze risks, Recommend recovery actions, Evaluate governance status and Generate executive operations report.

## Enterprise Controls

- Autonomous actions remain recommendation-first with approval workflows for material operational risk.
- Recovery workflows preserve tenant isolation, RBAC, audit trails and governance evidence.
- Self-healing suggestions are designed for operator review before critical execution.
- AI governance evolution links policy, audit, compliance, model monitoring and agent governance into one operations surface.
