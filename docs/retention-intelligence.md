# Retention Intelligence

Version: 0.29.0

Retention Intelligence tracks cohorts, churn signals, engagement scores and product stickiness so customer success, product and executive teams can act before risk becomes revenue loss.

## Capabilities

- Retention cohorts by workspace, segment and product usage.
- Engagement scores using activity, feature adoption and active days.
- Churn signal detection and stickiness analysis.
- AI recommendations for retention improvement.
- Product health and customer success context sharing.

## API Groups

- /v1/retention
- /v1/engagement
- /v1/growth-insights

## Database Models

- RetentionCohort
- EngagementScore
- GrowthRecommendation
- ProductHealthScore

## AI Context

Retention data feeds the AI Product Copilot and can be combined with customer health, revenue opportunities, service history and product events for contextual recommendations.
