# Phase 43 Analytics Coverage Report

Version: 0.43.0
Date: 2026-06-14

## Analytics Domains

- OEE analytics through calculateOee and ManufacturingMetric records.
- Yield analytics through completed and scrapped execution quantities.
- Quality analytics through QualityInspection and QualityIssue records.
- Maintenance analytics through MaintenanceAsset and MaintenancePrediction records.
- Cost analytics through ProductionCostRecord with material, labor, overhead and total cost.
- Risk analytics through ManufacturingRisk probability, impact and severity fields.
- Digital twin analytics through DigitalTwinModel and DigitalTwinSnapshot state, predictions and anomalies.
- Copilot analytics through ManufacturingCopilotQuery context and recommendation payloads.
