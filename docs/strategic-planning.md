# Strategic Planning

Version: 0.30.0

The Strategic Planning layer connects executive goals, KPI planning, department planning, budgets, capacity and AI Strategy Copilot recommendations.

## Capabilities

- Strategic plans with objectives, assumptions, departments and KPIs.
- Strategic goals with target metrics, milestones, current values and due dates.
- KPI planning and department planning APIs.
- Budget and capacity context for strategic execution.
- AI Strategy Copilot recommendations and risk alerts.

## API Groups

- /v1/strategic-planning
- /v1/budget-planning
- /v1/capacity-planning

## Database Models

- StrategicPlan
- StrategicGoal
- BudgetPlan
- CapacityPlan
