
# Spend Intelligence

Spend intelligence turns procurement activity into operating signals. It captures spend transactions, category insights, amount impact, confidence, findings and recommendations.

## Capabilities

- Spend transaction tracking by vendor, purchase order, category, cost center and status.
- Spend insights for variance, concentration, budget pressure and supplier risk.
- Budget allocation monitoring across periods, categories, departments and owners.
- Event-driven publication for spend transactions and insights.
