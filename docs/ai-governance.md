# AI Governance

Version: 0.32.0

Phase 32 adds the Enterprise AI Governance Center for policy, risk, compliance, auditability and governed AI lifecycle management.

## Capabilities

- AI Governance Center summary across model registry, ModelOps, decisions, compliance and performance.
- Governance policy and access policy registry metadata.
- AI risk monitoring across decision risks, model drift and degraded deployments.
- Auditability linking AI decisions, explanations and audit logs.

## API Groups

- /v1/ai-governance
- /v1/ai-audit
- /v1/ai-compliance
